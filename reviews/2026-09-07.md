## Decision and uncertainty

The athlete reports feeling ready. Asked about additional runs and current
shin/soleus pain; absent an answer, the prescription explicitly assumes no
additional running and pain-free legs. Pain-free status is not a confirmed fact.
Prior shin/soleus history and the running interruption govern the initial load.
40 km is an individualized coaching choice based on prior experience and recent
exposure, not a Garmin-derived safe-load threshold or a guaranteed safe amount.

Garmin readiness incorporates sleep, recovery time, HRV, acute load and recent
sleep/stress. Its positive score supports the recovery assessment; it does not
establish tolerance for an immediate return to pre-vacation mileage.
[Garmin training readiness](https://www.garmin.com/en-MY/garmin-technology/running-science-entry-level/after-running/training-readiness/).
Acute load reflects recent physiological exercise load, and load focus describes
its intensity distribution. We will not add hard intervals simply to clear the
anaerobic-shortage label.
