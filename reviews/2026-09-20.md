## Trends and interpretation

- Tuesday and Thursday again show the established tendency to convert Easy running
  into moderate running. HR averages remained below 148, but Thursday RPE 4–5 and
  Sunday's final 5 km RPE 4–5 demonstrate that HR below the ceiling does not by
  itself make a run easy.
- Sunday's 3 km lap averages rose from HR 122.9 to 147.7 while pace stayed about
  4:16–4:42/km. The first lap likely includes HR settling. Later laps remained near
  the Easy HR ceiling, but the faster-than-prescribed pace and rising RPE make this
  a moderate long run, not a clean easy-effort benchmark.
- Wednesday and Saturday were credible recovery runs by available effort data.
  Saturday lacks HR, so its physiological response cannot be inferred.
- The last two running weeks total 46.07 and 55.41 km, average 50.74 km. The rolling
  four-week mean is distorted downward by vacation (22.71, 0, 46.07, 55.41 km).
  Historical July/August 75–95 km weeks establish capacity, but do not justify a
  rapid return after the interruption.
- There was no formal threshold workout. The Thursday strides were tolerated, which
  supports a small quality reintroduction, not an immediate return to old volume or
  PB pace.

## Recovery interview and decision

The athlete reports no pain, no current fatigue, no physical load outside Strava,
and availability for 6–7 days. Saturday RPE was about 2; Sunday's final 5 km were
RPE 4–5. Sleep was poor throughout the week and HRV about 65 ms. Resting-HR trend
and fresh RUNALYZE/Garmin fitness markers are unavailable. The next-morning response
after Sunday is represented by the September 21 report of no pain or fatigue.

Poor sleep and lower HRV argue against aggressive progression. After the athlete requested 60–65 km, prescribe 61.5 km with two rest days and one short threshold session only if sleep improves and normal energy holds. If the sleep gate fails, replace it with 10 km Easy for a 60 km week; do not move the quality session later. The October 10 HM may be skipped or used as a controlled long
tempo, so no PB-specific race pace is prescribed this week.
