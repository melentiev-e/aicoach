## The threshold session — the key finding

Run in rain, with abdominal discomfort at "the edge of diarrhea," yet:

| Rep | Pace | HR (avg→max) |
|-----|------|--------------|
| 1 | 3:53 | 149 → 164 |
| 2 | 3:51 | 157 → 168 |
| 3 | 3:53 | 160 → 166 |
| 4 | 3:55 (last km ~4:00) | 160 → 169 |

- HR stayed **inside the guardrail (avg ≤165, max ≤170)** for all four reps — even the
  slower fourth rep only touched 169.
- Athlete's read: RPE was low enough that 1–2 more reps at this pace felt possible. That's
  a meaningful signal — under compromised conditions (illness + rain), 3:50–3:53/km at
  sub-max effort confirms the threshold correction from Wk6 (true MLSS ≈ 3:50/km) is
  holding, and there may be room to progress this band soon.
- **Don't overreact to one session** — hold the same pace/HR prescription one more cycle
  and let the pending phase-review (fresh RUNALYZE snapshot) formally re-anchor the zones
  rather than adjusting off a single good workout done in imperfect conditions.

## New flag: right soleus soreness/tightness (post-threshold)

- Reported after Friday's threshold; no mention of altered gait, so this stays in-house
  for now rather than a referral — but it sits directly upstream of this athlete's known
  shin-splint pattern (2–3×/year during peaking blocks), so treat it as an early warning,
  not a footnote.
- **Action this week:**
  - Continue/emphasize the existing calf-raise + tibialis-raise protocol, and add soleus-
    specific work: seated (bent-knee) calf raises and foam rolling/self-massage for the
    soleus, 2–3×/week.
  - Hold off on hills (which load the soleus eccentrically on the downhill) until it's
    confirmed pain-free — see Week 8 prescription below.
  - Flag daily: any tightness that changes stride mechanics or produces sharp/localized
    pain → stop and reassess per §8 (refer out).
