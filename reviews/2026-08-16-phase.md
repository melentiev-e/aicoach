## Trajectory read

Two true things that point in opposite directions:

1. **The engine is at the 2:39 ceiling.** HM prognosis **1:15:41 ≈ his HM PB (1:15:32)**.
   Riegel from the current HM *or* 10 k → marathon ≈ **2:38**. VO2max 62.3 and climbing,
   RHR 36, HRV 73. As a *race-equivalent engine*, he is where 2:39 lives.

2. **The marathon is shape-limited, and shape is not there yet.** Marathon Shape 43%
   (needs ~65%+ for 2:39). Goal MP 3:46/km currently **equals threshold pace** (this
   week's reps sat 3:44–3:49), i.e. it is not yet marathon-*sustainable*. The
   race-equivalent 2:38 assumes marathon-specific endurance is built to match the
   engine — Shape 43% says it is not. And the lactate caveat stands: 5.3→6.1 mmol at
   threshold means the pace-implied LT likely overstates true MLSS ([[lactate-response-high]]),
   so 3:46-as-MP is optimistic even before the shape gap.

So: **engine/ceiling ≈ 2:38; shape-limited current marathon ≈ 2:48–2:52; the Nov 1
result depends entirely on how much of the engine the compressed specific phase converts
into marathon-sustainable shape.**

## The downgrade trigger — resolved

The master plan set this trigger for today: *"if after Phase 2 (Aug 16) Garmin LT has not
moved below 3:32/km OR marathon shape < 45%, revise race target to 2:43–2:45."*

- LT ≈ 3:46–3:48/km — **not** below 3:32. ✗
- Marathon Shape 43% — **below 45%.** ✗

Both conditions for holding 2:39 fail. Honest call, and it matches where the Aug 3
review already leaned. **But** — Shape missed 45% by two points on a metric that just
rose nine, and the engine is genuinely at PB level. This is not "2:39 is off"; it is
"2:39 is a stretch that current marathon-specific fitness does not yet support."

## Decision

- **Primary target for Nov 1 → 2:43:00 (≈3:52/km).** This is the evidence-supported,
  shape-consistent number: it asks Shape to climb from 43% toward ~60–65% and MP (3:52)
  to become sustainable — a realistic ask over the compressed specific phase.
- **2:39 held as an explicit, live upside — not closed.** The engine supports it; the
  door re-opens at **Phase review 4 (~Oct 18)** *if* the specific phase delivers: Shape
  ≥ ~62%, MP 3:46 holding for 10+ km at HR ≤ 158, LT drifting toward 3:38. If those land,
  we race 2:39. If they land halfway, 2:41. We decide on specific-phase evidence, not today.
- **This is the disciplined checkpoint working, not a setback.** The plan built this
  fork deliberately so the call is made on data, not hope. The trajectory since April's
  dip is strongly positive; we're picking the honest number and keeping the ambitious one
  earned rather than assumed.
